guide to providence

option 1:

(morning) • start the day off at frank and laurie's (afternoon) • explore hope st. (stock, henry bear’s park, frog & toad, etc.) • walk along the providence river and the michael s. van leesten memorial bridge or • visit the risd museum (evening) • dinner in federal hill - federal hill is a historic neighborhood on the west side of providence, rhode island, best known as the city’s italian-american district *pastiche fine desserts and big feeling are close by

option 2:

(morning) • start the day off at amy’s (for a breakfast sandwich) and coffee exchange (for a drink) (afternoon) • explore wickenden st. (knit club, steward house, gallery belleau) • quick afternoon snack at pizza marvin - “one roni slice please” or “once soft serve please” • walk along the seekonk river at india point park or • visit the risd museum (evening) • dinner at al forno • walk along the providence river and the michael s. van leesten memorial bridge

a note from the designer: i wanted to create a handmade website that acts as a “guide to providence,” cataloging local stores, cafes, and restaurants. although sites and applications like google maps or yelp exist and help locals and visitors find stores and eateries, i wanted to create a curated list from the perspective of someone who lives in providence. over the past three years i’ve explored and have been exposed to the incredible charm of the city. i made a list of my must go spots and wanted to create a platform to share it with the greater providence community and tourists within the area. i hope you enjoyed! - katie kim
