a note from the designer:
i wanted to create a handmade website that acts as a “guide to providence,” cataloging local stores, cafes, and restaurants. although sites and applications like google maps or yelp exist and help locals and visitors find stores and eateries, i wanted to create a curated list from the perspective of someone who lives in providence. over the past three years i’ve explored and have been exposed to the incredible charm of the city. i made a list of my must go spots and wanted to create a platform to share it with the greater providence community and tourists within the area. i hope you enjoyed!
- katie kim
